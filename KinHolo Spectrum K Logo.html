Spectrum K

The mark for KinHolo. A single geometric monogram that carries the brand name, its promise, and its full palette in one calm shape.

The idea

A ring and a K, set as one mark, where the brand name, its promise and its palette all live together.

01

One geometric mark.

The ring and the K share a single stroke weight, cap height and rounded cap, so they read as one form rather than a shape set beside a letter.

02

K for Kin.

Three limbs from one spine: the people in a household, different and joined. It is the half of the mark that moves and reaches outward.

03

The ring is the whole.

Holos means whole. One protective circle, unbroken: a single private shared household, holding everyone safely inside it.

04

It still reads OK.

Read as letters, the mark says the quietest reassurance there is, in every language the app speaks: everything is handled, everyone is fine.
