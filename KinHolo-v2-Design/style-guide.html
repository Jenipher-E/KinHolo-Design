KinHolo v2 style guide

Design C, Daylight title card. The visual summary. Full rules are in STYLE-GUIDE.md and the values in tokens/tokens.json.

Principles

Five rules every screen follows.

  • Calm firstOne clear thing to do per screen. Colour is spent on meaning.
  • Show the householdLead with what is happening at home today, not with features.
  • Cinematic through framingTeal title bands and the household photo. No glows, blurs or gradient buttons.
  • White is the working surfaceContent sits on white sheets. Teal is for headers and primary actions.
  • Plain wordsSentence case, short sentences, no em dashes, no exclamation marks.

Colour

KinHolo colours only. Coral never sits behind text, and small coral words use the darker text shade.

        How KinHolo talks.

        • Say it like the household would"Tom can’t find basmati rice", "€316 left", "Nobody yet".
        • No em dashesUse a full stop, a comma or "and". No middle dots in sentences either.
        • Sentence caseHeadings and buttons included. No capitals for emphasis.
        • Plain money words"On track to go €40 over", never "pacing".
        • Privacy as reassurance"Only adults can see money", not warnings.
        • Names over roles"Ask Tom", not "Ask the other adult".
